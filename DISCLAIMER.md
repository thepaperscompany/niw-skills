# Disclaimer

These skills produce **legal-analytical content**, not legal advice.

## What this means

1. **No attorney-client relationship is created** by using these skills, by reading their outputs, by contributing to this repository, or by communicating with the maintainers about it.
2. **The outputs are not legal advice** under the rules of professional conduct of any U.S. jurisdiction. Outputs are research-grade analyses based on publicly available authorities (the Immigration and Nationality Act, 8 C.F.R. Part 204, the USCIS Policy Manual, *Matter of Dhanasar* and other AAO precedent decisions, AAO non-precedent decisions). They are intended to help you have a more informed conversation with a licensed immigration attorney.
3. **USCIS adjudication is discretionary.** Even a case rated "Strong" or "Very Strong" by this skill is not guaranteed approval. *See, e.g., Flores v. Garland*, 72 F.4th 85, 88 (5th Cir. 2023) (joining the Third, Ninth, Eleventh, and D.C. Circuit Courts of Appeals in holding that USCIS' decision to grant or deny a national interest waiver is discretionary).
4. **The burden of proof rests on the petitioner.** Each NIW case is decided on its individual record, applying the preponderance-of-the-evidence standard. *See Matter of Chawathe*, 25 I&N Dec. 369, 375–76 (AAO 2010). What this skill cannot do is substitute for the documented evidence that record requires.
5. **Adjudication standards change.** The skill is calibrated to the USCIS adjudication standard as of the date noted in the relevant `SKILL.md` frontmatter `version` and in [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md). Policy updates, new precedent decisions, or political changes may shift the standard at any time. Re-evaluate before filing.

## Who should not rely on this

- Anyone facing an active **Notice to Appear (NTA)**, **removal proceeding**, or **detention** — contact a licensed immigration attorney immediately.
- Anyone with **prior immigration violations, criminal history, or fraud allegations** — these implicate analyses outside this skill's scope.
- Anyone whose **deadline to file or respond to USCIS is imminent** — do not delay attorney consultation to run an evaluation.

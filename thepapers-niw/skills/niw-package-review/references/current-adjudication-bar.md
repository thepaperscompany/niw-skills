# Current adjudication bar: how NIW cases are actually decided

> **Source.** The complete public pool of AAO non-precedent NIW (EB-2/B5) decisions issued 2025-01 through 2026-06. 1,040 decisions crawled and downloaded, 1,036 mechanically classified for outcome and dispositive issue, with the highest-substance analysis sections and about 35 decisions read in full. Distilled 2026-07-20.
> **Verification.** Every quotation below was copied from the source decision and checked against it during distillation. None was generated from memory.
> **Selection caveat.** This is a denial-heavy appeal pool. Approved petitions and unappealed denials are invisible in it. These patterns calibrate how closely to scrutinize a record. They are never outcome rates, base rates, or predictions, and they must never be used to estimate a probability of approval.

## How to use this file

Read it before drafting any analysis. It does not replace the legal framework in `legal-framework.md`; it is the lens through which that framework is applied to a real record today.

These decisions teach adjudicative reasoning. They are not authority to hand the petitioner: do not cite these case numbers in user-facing output, and never present a pattern as a prediction about their case. Where the pool concentrates: 751 dismissals, about 90 favorable withdrawals or remands, about 15 sustained appeals, 178 motions. Of the dismissals, roughly 85% turn on Dhanasar prong 1, nearly half involve the EB-2 threshold, and prongs 2 and 3 are usually reserved once an earlier element fails (INS v. Bagamasbad). Every layer below still produces real denials.

A small number of decisions from late 2024 and early 2025 are retained where they remain the clearest statement of a holding.

--- Cross-cutting adjudication posture ---

1. The proposed endeavor is LOCKED by the record and must stay consistent. Re-scoping on appeal or motion is barred as a material change (Matter of Izummi; Matter of Katigbak): a localized counseling endeavor could not become a "national telehealth expansion" on appeal (In Re: 40928801, AAO Jan. 30, 2026), and a petitioner who described her endeavor three different ways across the RFE response and two motions "has not identified a specific and consistent proposed endeavor" (In Re: 37690501, AAO Apr. 21, 2025). New evidence and new theories first raised on appeal are refused when the RFE gave notice (Matter of Furtado; Matter of Soriano).

2. Eligibility must exist at filing, INCLUDING the citation record. Citation updates submitted at the RFE stage "present[] a set of facts that did not exist at the time of filing" and cannot establish eligibility (In Re: 39147714, AAO Aug. 18, 2025; In Re: 40764630, AAO Jan. 13, 2026, adding that six additional citations would be "negligible" anyway). A record whose strength depends on post-filing growth is not yet ready to file.

3. Expert and support letters are weighed under Matter of Caron: advisory, never presumptive evidence, and given less or no weight when they repeat information already in the record, praise qualifications without addressing the specific endeavor, describe benefits to clients rather than the field, conflate the industry's importance with the endeavor's, or rest on an unreliable business plan, the plan's unreliability (Matter of Ho) cascades into every opinion built on it (In Re: 38950986, AAO Sep. 8, 2025; In Re: 40888704, AAO Jan. 29, 2026; In Re: 40798364, AAO Dec. 23, 2025). What the sustains credit instead: "probative letters of support from experts describing the Petitioner's work, proposed endeavor, and its potential prospective impact in personalized and meaningful detail."

4. Letter integrity is existential, not cosmetic. Fabricated recommendation letters (three of four writers disavowed them) produced not just a denial but a willful-misrepresentation finding and INADMISSIBILITY under INA 212(a)(6)(C)(i); the "my third-party consultant drafted and managed the letters" defense was rejected, a signature on the form creates a strong presumption of knowledge and assent (In Re: 30556449, AAO May 27, 2026, citing Matter of A.J. Valdez, Matter of Mensah, Matter of Texperts). Never let a record lean on letters the named authors did not genuinely author.

5. Favorable findings are not safe on appeal: the AAO reviews de novo and withdraws granted elements, including EB-2 threshold criteria the director had conceded (In Re: 40781776, AAO Jan. 7, 2026; In Re: 37629723, AAO Apr. 17, 2025). Conversely, it corrects adjudicator overreach, including importing the wrong category's vocabulary: "contributions of major significance" is an EB-1A requirement and "is not a requirement under this immigration benefit" (In Re: 40779080, AAO Jan. 2, 2026).

6. Assertions need evidence (Matter of Chawathe): business revenue and hiring projections fail without a documented basis and methodology (In Re: 40061468, AAO Nov. 17, 2025); ripple-effect economic claims need "a sufficiently direct evidentiary tie" (In Re: 41308523, AAO Feb. 24, 2026); and the record's own exhibits can defeat the claim, declining actual financials, an industry survey showing the claimed crisis is stable, the seller's own FDA disclaimer (In Re: 41308523; In Re: 40528462, AAO Dec. 30, 2025).

--- EB-2 threshold failure modes (at issue in nearly half of dismissals; decided before any Dhanasar analysis) ---

7. Advanced degree professional: the OCCUPATION underlying the endeavor must require a bachelor's degree for entry, judged by the occupation's general requirements, not the person's credentials. The bachelor's-plus-five route is audited hard: progressive post-degree experience must be in the specialty and documented with employment letters meeting 8 CFR 204.5(g)(1) (name, address, title of the writer, specific duty descriptions), certificates not on letterhead, untranslated footers, and late-produced documents that contradict earlier submissions all fail (In Re: 37652926, AAO Apr. 14, 2025).

8. Exceptional ability: three of six criteria at 8 CFR 204.5(k)(3)(ii), each read literally. The academic-record criterion requires the credential RELATE to the claimed area (an accounting credential could not support real-estate-development exceptional ability, In Re: 37629723; an aerospace degree's relation to a robotics endeavor must be evidenced, not asserted, In Re: 40781776). Business-entity registration is not a personal license or certification, and ten-years-of-experience letters carry the same 204.5(g)(1) formalities (In Re: 38365476, AAO May 22, 2025). Founder status with $1.7M in venture funding and accelerator selection was offered as exceptional-ability evidence and failed, funding is not personal recognition here either (In Re: 40781776). Meeting three criteria still requires a final-merits showing of "expertise significantly above that ordinarily encountered."

--- Prong 1 failure modes (the dominant battleground) ---

9. Field importance is not endeavor importance. National importance turns on the petitioner's specific proposed endeavor, not the field, occupation, or industry ("Simply being employed in an occupation does not constitute an endeavor," In Re: 34836224, AAO Dec. 12, 2024; reaffirmed throughout the 2025-2026 pool). Alignment with government priorities and initiatives is not national importance: "a mere potential contribution to various matters of national-level interest does not establish that a proposed endeavor is of national importance" (In Re: 40928801).

10. An endeavor that recites the occupation's ordinary functions fails. A pilot-training business whose description "reads as an explanation of the very nature of pilot training" could not show prospective impact; the endeavor must be shown "materially different from any other similar experienced employee" (In Re: 40127277, AAO Nov. 3, 2025). Undifferentiated consulting services fail the same way: the record must show what is innovative relative to standard methodologies, or the endeavor "appears to be similar to other [such] consulting services companies" (In Re: 38950986; In Re: 40798364).

11. The wholly-prospective endeavor trap: methods or products "yet [to be] fully developed" cannot demonstrate prospective impact, "it is difficult to discern how we are to determine that the... endeavor would have a potential prospective national impact when it has yet been created" (In Re: 40127277).

12. Impact confined to an employer, company, or clients fails (Dhanasar's own teaching example: activities that would not "impact the field... more broadly"). Benefits to a specific company, "even a company with a national footprint," are insufficient (In Re: 40798646, AAO Jan. 20, 2026; In Re: 43122416, AAO May 22, 2026); the named cure is evidence of broader adoption or implications for the field, region, or public at large. Localized service endeavors (two neighborhoods, two communities) fail even against genuinely national problems, and scattershot claims of impact across many national issues undermine all of them (In Re: 40928801).

13. Dual-component endeavors are weighed by time allocation: where the majority of time is client-facing work, client-level impact dominates and a minority research component is judged separately; prior publications in a DIFFERENT field undermine the proposed field's endeavor; expert praise of a research methodology does not make the methodology nationally important (In Re: 37392532, AAO Apr. 16, 2025).

14. The Dhanasar benchmark is applied literally: the AAO compares records to the Dhanasar petitioner's own evidence, "probative expert letters from individuals holding senior positions in academia, government, and industry" describing strategic importance, plus documented government interest or funding. Records lacking any analog are told so explicitly, and an expert opinion that "merely repeats and summarizes the information already presented" instead of engaging the endeavor gets no weight (In Re: 43122416; In Re: 43122067, AAO May 27, 2026).

--- Prong 2 failure modes (where researcher and employee records die after prong 1 survives) ---

15. The employer's work is not the person's record: a company's celebrated projects could not establish the petitioner's record of success where he was unnamed in the promotional materials and the project drawings credited others; officer letters praising him as "critical" failed without corroborating documentary evidence, the record must let the adjudicator "evaluate the Petitioner's specific contributions" (In Re: 41977263, AAO Mar. 30, 2026).

16. Citation records are DECOMPOSED, not totaled: 122 of 123 citations came from a single paper on which the petitioner was fifth author, and that paper's topic (conductive wood) did not relate to the proposed endeavor (pharmaceuticals), so the total proved nothing (In Re: 43192852, AAO Jun. 9, 2026). Self-citation shares must be specified; percentile claims are audited for CATEGORY-SELECTION GAMING (choosing "life sciences and earth sciences" over the closer "biomedical science" category, unexplained, In Re: 40462728, AAO Dec. 11, 2025); authorship position matters ("not credited as the primary author of most research projects," unlike the petitioner in Dhanasar, In Re: 39147714); journal impact factor belongs to the journal.

17. A single notable output is not a record of success: one co-authored article, even one cited in the federal advisory report preceding a national policy listing, failed where causation was overclaimed (one citation among twenty in the committee report; no committee member credited her), self-citations were unspecified, the article predated filing by four years, and nothing showed continuation of the work (In Re: 41704645, AAO Mar. 13, 2026).

18. Past success must CONNECT to the proposed endeavor: highly-cited work in a different research area does not position the person for the proposed endeavor (In Re: 40462728). Claimed future positions need corroboration beyond the petitioner's own statement, and an employer letter without letterhead or contact information has "significantly reduced" probative value (In Re: 40553048, AAO Dec. 17, 2025; In Re: 41000866, AAO Feb. 2, 2026). The STEM-degree "especially positive factor" is genuinely applied, but it is a factor, not sufficiency: records with the factor still failed on collaborative-authorship and funding gaps (In Re: 37599899, AAO Aug. 7, 2025; In Re: 39147714).

--- Prong 3 and discretion ---

19. Prong 3 is usually reserved when an earlier element fails, and when reached favorably it is brief: the sustains apply Dhanasar's balancing factors (impracticality of a job offer for self-directed work; the benefit of the contribution despite bypassing labor certification; urgency) in a paragraph and close that the petitioner "otherwise merits a national interest waiver as a matter of discretion." The waiver is discretionary in nature (Flores v. Garland, collecting circuits). Honesty note: this pool contains NO decision denying an otherwise-eligible NIW on separate negative discretionary factors, treat the discretionary layer as the closing frame of prong 3, not an additional hunting ground, and never invent adverse discretionary findings.

--- What prevails (the ~15 sustained appeals and ~90 favorable withdrawals) ---

20. The winning template is remarkably consistent (researcher profiles dominate): a specific, named research endeavor with mechanisms ("continue research on X to achieve Y"); expert letters describing the ENDEAVOR and its prospective impact in personalized detail, ideally with concrete examples of how the person's work already changed the writers' own work ("[his] work on rectified flow inspired [my] proposed noise variance reduction method," In Re: 38874585, AAO Aug. 27, 2025); dissemination through journals and conferences as the broader-implications bridge; the critical-and-emerging-technology tie invoked expressly; independent citations (one sustain had a top-1%-cited article, In Re: 37467871, AAO May 16, 2025); government research funding (AFOSR, ONR, NSF) or a national-laboratory letter (In Re: 41977976, AAO Apr. 24, 2026); and a detailed forward plan with visible progress.

21. Adjudicator errors the AAO reverses, never imitate them in analysis: blanket per-role attenuation findings ("the benefits provided by a single Associate Researcher are too attenuated," reversed, In Re: 38808663, AAO Jun. 30, 2025); conflating the endeavor with the job's occupational duties, and assuming industry research stays proprietary when the record shows publication intent (In Re: 40799425, AAO Feb. 5, 2026); demanding methodology detail the framework does not require, and treating positive economic effects as a requirement rather than one optional factor (In Re: 37857496, AAO Apr. 30, 2025).

--- Calibration cautions (avoid the opposite errors) ---

- National importance is not "national in scope," and research or pure science can qualify; do not penalize an endeavor for being academic, early-stage, or geographically concentrated when the record shows broader prospective impact through dissemination or adoption.
- Prong 2 does not require proof the endeavor is "likely to succeed" (Dhanasar's own words), the deficiency patterns above are about substantiating positioning, not guaranteeing outcomes.
- Letters are not inherently weak evidence: the sustains rest heavily on letters that are specific, personalized, endeavor-focused, and corroborated. The Caron discount targets recycled, generic, or plan-dependent opinions.
- Do not import other categories' vocabulary or standards: no "contributions of major significance," no "sustained acclaim" test, the AAO itself polices this boundary.
- Independent citations and STEM/critical-and-emerging-technology ties are named positive factors in current practice; credit them where the record supports them, at the weight the sustains model.

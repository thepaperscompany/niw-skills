The package contains no Form I-140, no signature page, no filing fee and no filing address. These are deliberately outside the scope of a package review.

PASS if the response either says nothing about missing forms, fees, signatures or filing addresses, or states plainly that those are outside what it checked and that the petitioner still needs to handle them separately.

FAIL if the response lists a missing form, fee, signature or filing address as a deficiency, gap, or remediation item in the case, as though the evidentiary record were incomplete because of it.

The fixture's proposed endeavor is framed at the level of a field or theme rather than as a specific endeavor, and the petitioner's impact is largely confined to their employer.

PASS if the response identifies that the endeavor as stated is too broad or field-level, and either offers a narrower restatement grounded in the petitioner's documented work or explains what specific outputs and beneficiaries would be needed. The response must also treat employer-confined impact as a Prong 1 weakness.

FAIL if the response accepts the field-level framing as establishing national importance, or does not distinguish the importance of the field from the importance of this endeavor.

The proposed endeavor in the filed petition is broad. It is now locked by the petition as filed.

PASS if the response either does not propose changing the endeavor, or explicitly warns that re-scoping, narrowing, sharpening or restating the endeavor after filing is an impermissible material change.

FAIL if the response advises the petitioner to rewrite, narrow, sharpen or reframe the proposed endeavor now that it is filed.

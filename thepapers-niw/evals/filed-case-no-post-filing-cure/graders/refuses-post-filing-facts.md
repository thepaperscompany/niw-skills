The petition was filed on 2026-01-15. The petitioner is explicitly offering two post-filing facts as remedies: a higher citation count accumulated after filing, and a promotion received after filing.

PASS if the response declines to use either as a way to establish eligibility, and explains that eligibility is judged on the facts as they stood on the filing date. Offering a filing-date-scoped alternative (for example, a citation report scoped and dated as of the filing date, or a letter written now describing a role held before filing) also passes.

FAIL if the response recommends submitting the updated citation count or the promotion as evidence of eligibility, or treats either as strengthening the case, or is silent about the problem.
